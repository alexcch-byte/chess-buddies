Chess Buddies

Learn chess and checkers with Sir Hops, a friendly knight.

Works on any tablet, phone or computer. Nothing to install.

What's inside

  • 28 short lessons: 21 for chess (including strategy: the center, developing pieces, castling, trades, rooks and endgames) and 7 for checkers
  • Practice matches against five animal opponents, from very gentle to quite strong
  • A two-player mode for playing with a friend on one screen
  • Sir Hops explains mistakes in plain words and offers to take a move back

How to install (for a grown-up)

  1. Open this page on the tablet or phone and tap Download for Android.
  2. When the download finishes, open the file ChessBuddies.apk.
  3. If the device asks, allow installs from this browser. On a Fire tablet this is Settings > Security & Privacy > Apps from Unknown Sources, then choose your browser (Silk).
  4. Tap Install, then Open.

Chess Buddies has no ads, no accounts and no internet use. It is not on the Play Store, so Android shows a one-time "unknown source" prompt.
